production console The player's read of the live engine, embedded in the game client at a system you control (and reachable standalone at /console). Every figure is the engine's resolved value from the snapshot — the browser computes none (design.md §5); the two trend lines are the only client-side element, a rolling buffer of values the engine really sent. Tabs pick a tier, the row below a resource; goods with no local production/consumption show idle. The Syndicate licence roster and the hero's facility block are MOCK — design-ahead of an engine licence layer that does not exist yet; they are tagged on screen and send nothing.
